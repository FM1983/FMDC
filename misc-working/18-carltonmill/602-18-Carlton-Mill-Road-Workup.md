## 1. Executive summary & recommendation

602/18 Carlton Mill Road is the **two-level penthouse** of the *Kamahi Apartments* — a small (13-unit), recently fully-renovated and **earthquake-strengthened to 100% NBS** boutique complex on the Avon River edge of Merivale, directly opposite Hagley Park and ~5 min to the Christchurch CBD. It is a **completed, prime-location lifestyle/owner-occupier asset**, not a development play.

- **Asking:** Offers Over **$1,449,000** (listed 10 Feb 2026; realestate.co.nz currently shows "By Negotiation"). **HomesEstimate $1.43M–$1.6M** (28 May 2026) — i.e. the ask sits at the bottom of the automated range, which is mildly encouraging but the AVM range is wide.
- **Scale:** ~**210 m²** over two levels, **2 bed / 2 bath + separate study**, **3 private decks**, **2 secure underground car parks**, double glazing, 3 heat pumps + underfloor heating in bathrooms.
- **Implied rate:** ≈ **$6,900/m²** at the $1.449M ask — a clear penthouse premium over the rest of the building (lower 2-bed units are listed $789k–$850k). Justifiable for the top-floor / dual-level / river-park aspect, but it is the price-setter for the building, so there are no higher in-building comps to support it.
- **Yield:** This is **not a yield asset.** On an estimated rental of ~$800–$900/week **[VERIFY against PropertyFiles doc 4 – Rental Appraisal]**, gross yield is ≈ **2.8–3.2%** before body-corp and rates — typical of a trophy penthouse. The case is location, scarcity and capital quality, not cash-on-cash return.

**Recommendation:** **Proceed to Stage 2 due diligence.** The location and the 100% NBS / full-refurb status materially de-risk what is normally the biggest concern with post-2011 Christchurch apartments (seismic and remediation). Before committing, resolve the four "swing" items: (i) **body-corporate financial health, LTMP and levy**, (ii) **insurance — full replacement + EQ cover**, (iii) **rates and RV**, and (iv) **whether the property is already under offer** (a Harcourts duplicate listing shows a "Sold" tag — see §6). Recommend a **conditional offer** (LIM, BC records, builder's report, insurance confirmation, finance) at or modestly below the $1.449M ask, anchored to the in-building price ladder.

## 3. Pricing & valuation analysis

- **Ask vs AVM.** $1,449,000 ask vs HomesEstimate $1.43M–$1.6M. The ask is ~1% above the bottom of the range and ~10% below the top. AVMs are unreliable for unique penthouses (thin comp set), so treat as a sanity check only, not a value anchor.
- **$/m².** ≈ $6,900/m² (ask ÷ 210 m²). For reference, prime new Christchurch apartment product and refurbished Merivale stock typically transacts $6,000–$8,500/m² for premium floors; a fully-renovated top-floor dual-level unit with river/park aspect and two car parks sits credibly in the upper half of that band.
- **In-building ladder (current listings).** 201/18 asking **$789k** (2bd/1ba/1 car); 401/18 and 2/18 **EOI over ~$849k–$850k**; 202/18, 301/18, 1/18 also listed. The penthouse at ~$1.45M is **~70–85% above** the standard 2-bed floors — a large but not unreasonable penthouse premium given it is roughly double the floor area, has a second bathroom, second car park, the study and the only two-level / top-floor / triple-deck configuration in the building.
- **Negotiation read.** "Offers Over" + a wide AVM + the bottom-of-range ask + a possible prior "Sold" flag on a duplicate listing suggests the vendor may be testing the market or re-listing. **Confirm live status first** (§6) before investing in paid DD.

**Confirm before offer:** the actual **RV** and **2019/2022 revaluation** position (doc 08), and whether the ask reflects a recent price adjustment from the Feb 2026 launch.
