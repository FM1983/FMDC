RE:Arch turned to face McLeans.

The most file-active workstream in the estate this week — restructured, its concept paper closed at v10, and its centre of gravity moved from the four Christchurch editions to McLeans heritage research.

What moved

The control plane was collapsed on 31 August from fourteen documents to four (D-023), and the decision register ran from D-018 to D-032 in under a fortnight — eleven of the fourteen new rulings landing on 31 Aug–1 Sep with FM personally in session both days. D-024 closed the Master Concept Paper at v10 — 52 MB, full provenance, never edited in place. The founding-partner deck is superseded: "recoverable for ideas; never for framing."

D-028 is the ruling that changes the proposition: McLeans is a major Citadel project with its own strategy — RE:Arch is an adjacent platform that will hold a permanent home in the Mansion as one occupant, and its abstract functions are not to colonise the building programme. That reverses the earlier "mothership" framing outright. Meanwhile the real work went to 70-RESEARCH: the McLeans research session built the largest verified body in the programme — a 735-row fact ledger, a 52-entry adjudicated contradiction register, a 551-row sourced chronology running 1819–2026, some 300 actors, 16 transcriptions.

The one Collection output of the week was a decline — 29/868 Colombo St ruled out as a CH—004 edition (complex ceiling $530k against the Capital Paper's $850k with no comparable; a 70% short-stay model likely non-complying) — and the analysis caught a $65,217 GST overstatement in the Capital Paper on the way through. The first real numeric underwriting output the platform has produced, and it said no.

What it cost, and what's still open

The 28 Aug bootstrap "materialised" the source tree and copied 205,759 files — 98.2% of them npm packages — plus a stale 621 MB copy of live Armada-Structure. It has been cleaned (workstream now 1.2 GB / 476 files; a 2 GB archive awaits FM's disposal ruling), and the episode minted the doctrine line "a derivative work product is never a source."

FM also caught the fourth false assurance in the control record: "the public Replit site is live" propagating into an external document when the site is password-gated — new rule: assert on returned content, never on a status code. Still open: server-side auth (AUTH-001, next slice, not started), render provenance, the CH—003 demolition call, and a CI collision where two workflows fire on the same trigger.

The un-promoted doctrine

Ten new doctrine rules were minted this fortnight — planning stops, point-never-copy, the move-vs-copy test, one-page-note discipline ("a one-pager is an audit of the record") — and none has been promoted to group doctrine: Citadel-AI's MASTER-DOCTRINE has not been touched since 16 July. The learning is real and it is stranded in one lane.

RE:Arch is absent from _MASTER-TRACKER entirely (stale since 25 Aug), and the RE:Arch legal entity remains unresolved — sponsor Citadel Capital, asset holder Imperator Holdings.

Annex 04 · McLeans — PropCo / OpCo

Incentive becomes equity.

The operating model for the Mansion is now fully articulated: no leases, no guarantees — operating partnerships bound, in FM's words, "not by leases, and guarantees and obligations, but by a shared, aligned vision."

The mechanics, as ruled

Each operation — hospitality, design retail, art — runs in a joint OpCo held 50/50 between the PropCo and the operator (D21: the PropCo holds, not Citadel, so operator profit flows straight to the building's balance sheet). The flip: under a conventional lease, landlord contributions are incentives written off; here the fitout is funded in the project cost, owned by the PropCo on completion, and the operator's contribution defrays that cost — pay-to-play, notionally $500k Cazador, $350k Someday, $100k Foenander — converting dollar-for-dollar into PropCo equity at the $1.00 occupier strike (consultants defer at $0.75).

Rent is turnover-pegged and capped at market, with no floor — and for every dollar of rent the PropCo receives, the operator receives the same dollar as an executive management fee: the matched pair. Profit beyond that splits 50/50. The model's own invariance, proven across the worksheets: PropCo total return = operator total return = cover ÷ 2, whatever base, whatever percentage, through the cap, through the collar.

Four instrument layers — OpCo equity, turnover rent, PropCo scrip, milestone equity — and the standing rule that they are never totalled. A percentage or a dollar without its instrument named is a defect.

Annex 05 · Ravenwood · Cuesko · the 27 August meetings

A handshake the record never caught.

The construction structure was overturned at 13:44, pitched at 15:00 and 16:00, and accepted in principle by 20:31 — and the only record of that acceptance is a phone capture filed to the wrong matter.

The overturn, and the vanishing ruling

B006 (26 Aug) had settled Ravenwood as a memo line — 12.5% inside Citadel's half, off the register. At 13:44 on 27 August FM overturned it: Armada Build 50/50, Chancellor targeted (not approached) for one half, the other half a Citadel-led holding company held four ways at 25% each — Citadel, Ravenwood, Cutfield, CPMC — real shares, on a register. The consequence nobody has written down: Citadel's share of the construction entity fell from 37.5% to 12.5%. The ruling has no D-number, no source capture — it exists only in dispatch-ledger rows and inside the deck, whose files were still being rebuilt at 15:21, twenty-one minutes into the meeting they were built for.

The only outcome record is FM's 20:31 capture: "onboard in principle (details to be worked out). They are excited to join our collective." It was filed to Armada-Fields/ARMADA-TEAM — not to the build lane, which has not gained a single file since 27 Aug. No minutes, no transcript, no email to or from any party; the deck was never emailed to anyone. Chancellor and James Law: still not approached as at 1 Sep.

G3 got worse, and Rev H made it concrete

The Cuesko independence gate was ruled "CUESKO WITHOUT GREG" — Greg Cutfield's move is personal, the firm stays as the independent build-price check, subject to asking Cuesko, which nobody has done. But the person who crossed on 27 August was Jono Cutfield — a serving Cuesko Senior Associate Director and the Cuesko hand on McLeans — the exact failure mode the conversation brief predicted by name ("Jonathan will handle it, he is separate from me… a cost report signed Cutfield fails the test on the face of the signature"). Two Cuesko people are now on the build side; the gate still reads as if only Greg were moving; the deck offered the board seat to Greg and the yes came from Jono. Whether the two are related is inference and stays unasserted.

Then on 28 August the Guiding Document Rev H went to fourteen recipients including both Cutfields and CPMC — still carrying the superseded memo-line structure, naming Greg only, not disclosing the four-way holdco in which CPMC itself is a proposed 25% holder, and stating the independence conflict about Greg, to Greg and Jono, in writing.

Nothing is contractual

The deck's own face: "Proposed and subject to discussion. Nothing agreed with anybody." Armada Build and the holdco do not exist; no consideration is recorded for any of the four holdings; "no entity can self-perform today." The in-principle handshake is real momentum — and it is resting entirely on one evening voice capture.
