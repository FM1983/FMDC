# Summary — 3370 GNR Mixed Residential Scheme (output 008)

A Citadel concept-development pack presenting the **recommended development concept** for
the 809 m² THAB site at 3370 Great North Road, Glen Eden: **26 design-led residences** in
a cost-efficient four-level brutalist walk-up (no lift, ~13 m) with brand-led interiors
and shared "colliding facilities". The scheme deliberately blends **two products — 16
single one-bedroom residences ($499,000) and 10 dual-key residences ($599,000, each two
lockable ~20 m² studios)** — to reach two buyer pools (owner-occupiers and yield
investors) and de-risk the sell-down. It supersedes the exploratory 006 (dense apartment)
and 007 (co-living) schemes as the lead concept.

**Adopted base case (ex-GST, $605k land entry):** total development cost **$9,144,378**,
gross realisation $13,974,000 incl GST, **development profit $3,006,927**, **margin
24.7%**, **ROE 142%**, residual land value **$1,761/m²** — above the $1,400/m² acquisition
ask (+$361/m² cushion). The dual-key out-yields the one-bed (5.6% vs 5.0% gross), so its
price is income-justified.

**The honest core.** Build cost is the swing variable — a $500/m² move in the dual-key
rate shifts margin ~2–3 points and land ~$400/m². Six flags carried, not softened: prices
are high for Glen Eden with no confirmed local comparable; dual-key build cost needs a QS
number; council treatment of dual-key consent needs an early planner's view; design
premiums are weaker outside transport-central locations; a fresh resource consent is
required (+$110k, 6–12 months); and a build-to-hold model should run alongside
build-to-sell. Recommended for advancement to concept design **subject to conditions
precedent**, not "proceed".

**Honesty rule.** Every visual (site plan, axonometric massing, stacking, unit plates,
elevation) is an indicative schematic diagram only — not to scale, not for construction or
consent. Interiors credited to Studio 11:11; building architect "to be appointed".

**Deliverables (output 008, 2026-06-25):** primary PDF `3370 Great North Road - Mixed
Residential Scheme.pdf` (11 pages) + this markdown twin + summary.

**Project framing.** The live transaction thesis is a capital-light, vendor-financed flip
(on-sell to a third party at ~$1,200/m²); this development concept underpins the land
value the end buyer is acquiring and is the fallback / buyer-facing case, not the base
transaction plan.
