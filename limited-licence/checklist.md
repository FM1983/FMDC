# Limited Licence — Filing Checklist (Monday 11 May 2026)

## Saturday 9 / Sunday 10 May — preparation

- [ ] Surrender physical licence card to NZTA per officer's instruction
      (s.30 LTA 1998). Keep proof of surrender.
- [ ] **Do not drive.** Any driving = s.32 offence (up to 3 months prison
      / $4,500 fine, 28-day vehicle impoundment).
- [ ] Order an Uber / arrange lifts for the weekend.
- [ ] Fill placeholders in `application.md` and `affidavit.md`:
  - [ ] Occupation, employer name and address
  - [ ] Years at current address
  - [ ] Licence classes / endorsements held
  - [ ] Working hours and routine driving needs
  - [ ] Public-transport realities (specific routes / times)
  - [ ] Ride-share cost estimate
  - [ ] Other licensed drivers in household and why they can't help daily
  - [ ] Family / dependant transport obligations (if any)
  - [ ] Year first held full licence; prior offences (be candid)
  - [ ] Remedial steps taken / proposed
  - [ ] Phone and email for the application footer
- [ ] **Confirm there is no prior s.90 suspension or disqualification in
      the last 5 years** — if there is, s.103(2)(d) may bar a limited
      licence and you need urgent legal advice.
- [ ] Email employer (Saturday) requesting two letters by close of Sunday:
  - **Letter A** — confirms role, ordinary hours, the driving the role
    requires, and that driving is essential to the position.
  - **Letter B** — sets out the consequences for the employer / business
    if you cannot drive for three months (loss of role, redeployment
    not feasible, third-party impact).
- [ ] Enrol in an AA Defensive Driving Course (online or in-person) and
      keep the receipt — it goes in the affidavit as a remedial step.
- [ ] Print **3 copies** of: application, affidavit, schedule of terms,
      annexures. Plus 1 file copy.
- [ ] Have the affidavit sworn / affirmed before a solicitor or JP on
      Sunday or first thing Monday. Auckland Citizens Advice Bureau and
      most law firms will swear an affidavit free or for a small fee. The
      court Registrar can also take it on the day.

## Monday 11 May — filing day

- [ ] **Auckland District Court**, 65–69 Albert Street, Auckland 1010.
      Counter open 9.00am.
- [ ] Bring: 3 sets of documents, photo ID, the suspension notice (POL
      1006 No. 1180703), payment for the filing fee.
- [ ] **Filing fee:** approx. **$498.50** (verify on the day; fees adjust
      1 July). Pay by EFTPOS / credit card / bank cheque.
- [ ] Ask Registry for the court's pro-forma "Application for Limited
      Licence" cover sheet and attach our application + affidavit as the
      grounds.
- [ ] Obtain a sealed copy stamped with the CRI/MISC number and hearing
      date.
- [ ] **Service** (same day):
  - [ ] Walk a sealed copy across to the Police Prosecutor's office at
        the Auckland District Court — get a stamped acknowledgement.
  - [ ] Email a sealed PDF to NZTA at legal@nzta.govt.nz (verify address
        with court Registry on the day) and post a hard copy to NZTA,
        Private Bag 11777, Palmerston North 4442. Keep tracking number.
- [ ] File an Affidavit of Service once both respondents are served.

## Between filing and hearing

- [ ] Continue not to drive.
- [ ] Complete the Defensive Driving Course; obtain certificate of
      completion to produce at the hearing.
- [ ] Prepare a one-page hearing note for the Judge: who you are, the
      hardship, the terms sought, the remedial steps taken. Hand copies
      to the bench, prosecutor, and Registry.
- [ ] If Police indicate they will oppose, consider engaging counsel for
      the hearing.

## After the order

- [ ] Take the sealed order to NZTA / an AA driver-licensing agent to
      have the limited licence issued (cannot be issued before
      6 June 2026).
- [ ] Carry the limited licence and a copy of the order whenever
      driving. Keep the journey logbook.
- [ ] Diarise the suspension end date — **8 August 2026** — and apply
      for full reinstatement.

## Risks / things that can go wrong

- **s.103(2)(d) bar** — prior suspension in last 5 years would defeat
  the application entirely. Confirm before filing.
- **Police opposition** — if Police argue the hardship is insufficient
  or the conduct is too serious, expect a contested hearing.
- **Hearing date after 6 June** — if the Court allocates a date weeks
  past 6 June, the limited licence is effectively delayed; ask Registry
  for the earliest date and explain the urgency.
- **Driving in the meantime** — a single s.32 offence will torpedo the
  application and add a fresh disqualification.
