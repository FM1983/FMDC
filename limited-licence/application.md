## Notice of Application

TAKE NOTICE that the Applicant applies to this Honourable Court for an order
under section 105 of the Land Transport Act 1998 authorising the New Zealand
Transport Agency (Waka Kotahi) to issue a limited licence to the Applicant on
such terms and conditions as the Court thinks fit.

The application is made on the grounds that:

1. The Applicant's driver licence (No. **DB052958**) was suspended for three
   (3) months by Notice No. **1180703** issued under section 90 of the Land
   Transport Act 1998 and served on the Applicant at 12:05am on 9 May 2026
   at Auckland Central.

2. The suspension is not one of the disqualifications excluded from limited
   licence relief by section 103(2) of the Land Transport Act 1998.

3. The 28-day stand-down period prescribed by section 103(1)(b) of the Land
   Transport Act 1998 will expire on **5 June 2026**.

4. The Applicant will suffer **extreme hardship**, and other persons (in
   particular the Applicant's *[employer / staff / dependants — see
   affidavit]*) will suffer **undue hardship**, if the Applicant is not
   permitted to drive during the balance of the suspension period, on the
   grounds set out in the Affidavit of Farhad Moinfar filed herewith.

5. The Applicant has not been disqualified or had a licence suspended on any
   previous occasion within the period of five (5) years immediately
   preceding the commencement of the current suspension. *[CONFIRM — if
   incorrect, see s.103(2)(d) — limited licence not available where prior
   s.90 suspension or disqualification within 5 years.]*

The orders sought are:

(a) An order under section 105 of the Land Transport Act 1998 authorising
    the issue of a limited licence to the Applicant for the balance of the
    suspension period, commencing on 6 June 2026 and expiring on 8 August
    2026; and

(b) Such terms and conditions as to the purpose, days, hours, area, vehicle
    class, and any other matter as the Court considers appropriate.

The terms sought by the Applicant are set out in the schedule to the
supporting affidavit.

## Notes for filing (delete before lodgement)

- File at District Court at Auckland, 65–69 Albert Street, Auckland 1010.
  Counter hours 9.00am–4.30pm Monday–Friday. Filing fee currently
  **$498.50** (verify on the day — fees adjust 1 July annually).
- The court provides its own pro-forma "Application for Limited Licence"
  cover sheet — ask the Registry for it on filing; this document is the
  body / grounds.
- Bring **three copies**: one for the Court file, one for service on Police,
  one for service on NZTA. Keep a fourth as your file copy.
- Service: Police prosecutor at Auckland District Court will usually accept
  service over the counter; NZTA accepts service by post or via
  legal@nzta.govt.nz (confirm on filing day).
- A hearing date will typically be allocated 3–5 weeks out, ideally landing
  on or shortly after 6 June 2026 so the order can take immediate effect.
