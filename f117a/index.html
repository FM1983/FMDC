FLYING

LEFT STICK — pitch & roll. THR slider — engines. Rotate off the runway above 160 KT. Watch the HUD: speed (left), altitude (right), heading tape (top). Below 150 KT you stall.

STEALTH

The EMV bar (bottom centre) shows how visible you are to radar. Enemy radar rings are on the MAP. Bay doors open, gear down and high altitude make you glow on their scopes. Fly LOW between radar rings. If the alert bar fills: SAM launch. Break lock with hard turns, terrain and CHF/FLR. Low and close, AAA guns hose tracers at you — jink or climb.

ATTACKING

Pick your bay loadout at the briefing. TGT cycles targets, WPN selects weapon. LGB (laser bomb) for ground targets — wait for IN RANGE, then FIRE (bay opens automatically — you WILL be visible). AGM-88 HARM kills radar/SAM emitters from 18 KM: shoot the rings down and walk in. AIM-9 for MiGs. The camera rides your weapon to impact — press VIEW to skip back.

VIEWS & FUEL

VIEW cycles cockpit / chase / orbit. Watch the FUEL bar — below BINGO, head home or you'll flame out and glide. Time ×4 (T button) helps on long legs; it auto-cancels near threats.

GETTING HOME

Follow waypoints (diamond on HUD, MAP for the big picture). ILS bars appear on final with gear down. Land: under 200 KT, gentle sink — or press AUTO within 14 KM, and ride it in.
