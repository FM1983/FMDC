FLYING

LEFT STICK — pitch & roll. THR slider — engines. Rotate off the runway above 160 KT. Watch the HUD: speed (left), altitude (right), heading tape (top). Below 150 KT you stall.

STEALTH

The EMV bar (bottom right) shows how visible you are to radar. Enemy radar rings are on the MAP. Bay doors open, gear down and high altitude make you glow on their scopes. Fly LOW between radar rings. If the alert bar fills: SAM launch. Break lock with hard turns, terrain and CHF/FLR.

ATTACKING

TGT cycles targets, WPN selects weapon. LGB (laser bomb) for ground targets — wait for IN RANGE, then FIRE (bay opens automatically — you WILL be visible). AIM-9 for MiGs.

GETTING HOME

Follow waypoints (diamond on HUD, MAP for the big picture). Land on your runway: gear down, under 200 KT, gentle descent — or press AUTO within 8 KM of home base, lined up, for autoland.
