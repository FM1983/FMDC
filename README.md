## Critical reminders

- **Do NOT drive** between now and the limited licence taking effect. Driving
  while suspended is an offence under s.32 LTA 1998 — **first offence: up to
  3 months' imprisonment or fine up to $4,500**, plus 28-day vehicle
  impoundment. Second offence: minimum 6-month disqualification.
- Surrender of the physical licence card to NZTA is required under s.30 LTA
  1998 within the time the officer specified on service.
- These documents are **drafts** prepared at short notice. Strongly recommend
  a 30-minute review with a traffic-law solicitor before filing — Auckland
  practitioners who do this frequently include Mark Sayers, Steve Cullen,
  and the duty solicitor at Auckland District Court can also assist.
