## Request for review of demerit-point record and supporting notice history — Driver Licence DB052958 / Suspension Notice 1180703

I write urgently in relation to a Notice of Driver Licence Suspension for
Excess Demerit Points (POL 1006, Notice No. **1180703**) served on me by
Police at 12:05am on 9 May 2026. The notice records that the Agency's
records show I have accumulated **115 demerit points** within the preceding
two-year period, triggering a three-month suspension under section 90 of
the Land Transport Act 1998.

I do not, by this letter, defy the operation of section 90; I am not driving
and have lodged an application for a limited licence in the District Court
at Auckland on today's date. However, I am surprised by the figure of 115
points and by the fact that the roadside notice was the first formal
indication I received that I was anywhere near the 100-point threshold.
For that reason I ask the Agency to:

### 1. Provide my full demerit-point record

Please provide, as soon as possible and in any event within 20 working days,
a complete copy of my demerit-point record covering the period **9 May
2024 to 9 May 2026**, including for each entry:

(a) the offence date;
(b) the offence description and the section / regulation breached;
(c) the points imposed;
(d) the means by which the offence was detected (officer-issued,
    speed-camera, red-light camera, etc.);
(e) the date the infringement notice was issued;
(f) the **address to which** the infringement notice was sent;
(g) the date (if any) on which the points were recorded against my
    licence; and
(h) any subsequent withdrawal, waiver, or correction.

### 2. Provide copies of all warning / threshold correspondence

Please also provide copies of any correspondence the Agency has sent to me
in the last two years concerning my demerit-point balance, including in
particular:

(a) any **50-point warning letter** issued under the Agency's standard
    practice; and
(b) any 100-point notice or other communication advising that suspension
    was imminent or had occurred.

For each such item please confirm the date sent and the postal or email
address used.

### 3. Confirm my recorded address

Please confirm the postal and electronic addresses recorded against

### 4. Review and reconsideration

If, on the strength of the record produced, it appears that:

(a) any infringement notice was sent to an address other than the one

(b) any infringement notice was not in fact delivered or received and was

(c) any points were recorded contrary to the Land Transport (Offences and

that the cumulative two-year total be recalculated accordingly. If the

Without limiting the scope of the review I have requested, I record that:

(a) I had **no notice** from the Agency that I had reached or was

(c) Any infringement notice not properly served on me at my recorded

    notice should not be counted toward the section 90 threshold.

## Notes (delete before sending)

- **Legal effect:** This letter does **not** suspend the operation of the
  s.90 suspension. Do not drive while waiting for the response.
- The 50-point warning letter is administrative practice, not a statutory
  pre-condition (*Cassidy v Police* and similar). Lack of it doesn't, on
  its own, invalidate the suspension. The real leverage is whether any
  individual infringement notices were not properly served at the
  recorded address — if so, the points referable to those notices can be
  challenged and the cumulative total may fall below 100.
- Send by email AND tracked post; keep the tracking number.
- If the Agency does not respond within 20 working days to the OIA
  component, escalate to the Ombudsman.
