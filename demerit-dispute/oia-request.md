Dear OIA team,

This is a request under the **Official Information Act 1982** and, to the
extent any of the information requested is "personal information" about
me, also under the **Privacy Act 2020** (Information Privacy Principle 6).

I am the holder of New Zealand driver licence number **DB052958** and the
recipient of Notice of Driver Licence Suspension No. **1180703** issued
on 9 May 2026. I request the following information:

1. The complete record held by the Agency of demerit points imposed
   against driver licence DB052958 for the period 9 May 2024 to 9 May
   2026, showing for each entry:
   (a) the offence date and description;
   (b) the points imposed;
   (c) the means of detection (officer / camera / other);
   (d) the date the infringement notice was issued;
   (e) the postal or electronic address to which it was sent;
   (f) the date the points were recorded; and
   (g) any waiver, withdrawal, or correction.

2. Copies of all correspondence sent by the Agency to me, or purportedly
   to me, regarding my demerit-point balance during that period,
   including any warning letter issued at or around the 50-point
   threshold and any communication issued at or around the 100-point
   threshold. Please confirm the date sent and address used for each
   item.

3. The postal and electronic addresses recorded against licence DB052958
   throughout the two-year period, with the dates of any change.

4. A copy of the Agency's **internal policy or operating procedure**
   governing:
   (a) the issue of demerit-point warning letters;
   (b) the calculation of the two-year demerit-point total for the
       purposes of section 90 of the Land Transport Act 1998; and
   (c) the procedure where an infringement notice is returned
       undelivered or where the Agency has reason to believe a notice
       has not been received by the licence-holder.

5. The Agency's record of the **service or non-service** of each
   infringement notice that contributed to my demerit-point total in
   the two-year period (including any returned mail, redelivery
   attempts, or reminder notices).

I ask that the response be sent to *[EMAIL]*. I am content to receive
the information in electronic form.

If any part of the request can be answered more quickly than the rest,
please release that information first under section 15(1)(a) of the
Act. The matter is urgent because a limited-licence application has
been filed in the District Court at Auckland and the records sought
are needed for that proceeding.

If the Agency proposes to withhold any information, please cite the
specific section of the Act relied on and explain how the public
interest in withholding outweighs the public interest in disclosure
under section 9(1).
